# Myntra Wishlist Decision Assistant — Problem Statement

## Goal
Improve **Wishlist → Purchase Conversion within 30 days** for Myntra.

---

## Context
This is a Product Management case study for **Myntra**. Based on AI discovery, survey responses, and user interviews, the following core problem was identified.

---

## Problem Statement

> **High-intent fashion shoppers often save products they genuinely like but delay purchasing because they lack enough trusted, decision-ready information to confidently evaluate the product and choose it over alternatives.**

Users currently compensate by:
- Reading many reviews
- Checking customer photos
- Comparing several wishlisted products
- Checking other shopping platforms
- Sometimes ordering and returning products when they are uncertain about quality, fit, or appearance

---

## Proposed Solutions — Myntra Wishlist Decision Assistant

### 1. Help Me Choose — AI Wishlist Comparator

Allow users to select **2–4 items** from their own wishlist and compare them side by side based on:

- Price
- Rating
- Size availability
- Fit feedback
- Quality
- Customer photos
- Delivery date
- Return policy
- Discounts

AI should give a simple recommendation such as **"Best overall choice"** and clearly explain why.

---

### 2. AI Review Summary

Use AI to summarize verified customer reviews into simple sections such as:

- Fit
- Quality
- Colour accuracy
- Fabric
- Value for money
- Common complaints

The summary should be short and easy to understand. Users should also be able to see the actual reviews behind the AI summary to maintain trust.

---

### 3. Product Confidence Card

Create a compact card for each wishlisted item that brings the most important purchase information into one place:

- Overall rating
- Number of reviews
- Customer photos
- Fit feedback
- Quality sentiment
- Colour accuracy
- Size availability
- Delivery estimate
- Return policy
- Current price and discount

The goal is to reduce the effort users spend jumping between multiple sections or external platforms before making a purchase decision.

---

## Desired User Journey

**Wishlist → Compare → Understand Reviews → Gain Confidence → Add to Bag → Purchase**

---

## Metrics

| Metric Type | Metric |
|---|---|
| **Primary Product Metric** | Wishlist → Add-to-Bag Conversion |
| **Business Metric** | 30-Day Wishlist → Purchase Conversion |
| **Guardrail Metrics** | Return Rate and Cancellation Rate |

---

## Design Guidelines

- Design as a realistic **Myntra mobile experience**, not an analytics dashboard.
- Keep the UI **clean, fashion-focused, modern, lightweight, and trustworthy**.
- Start with the **Wishlist screen**, then design the **Help Me Choose flow**, **AI Review Summary**, and **Product Confidence Card**.
