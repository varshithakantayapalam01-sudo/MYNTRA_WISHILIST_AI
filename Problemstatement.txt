Use this prompt:

> I am working on a Product Management case study for **Myntra** with the goal of improving **Wishlist → Purchase Conversion within 30 days**.
>
> Based on AI discovery, survey responses, and user interviews, I identified this problem:
>
> **High-intent fashion shoppers often save products they genuinely like but delay purchasing because they lack enough trusted, decision-ready information to confidently evaluate the product and choose it over alternatives.**
>
> Users currently compensate by reading many reviews, checking customer photos, comparing several wishlisted products, checking other shopping platforms, and sometimes ordering and returning products when they are uncertain about quality, fit, or appearance.
>
> I want to design a **Myntra Wishlist Decision Assistant** focused on these 3 solutions:
>
> **1. Help Me Choose — AI Wishlist Comparator**
> Allow users to select 2–4 items from their own wishlist and compare them side by side based on:
>
> * Price
> * Rating
> * Size availability
> * Fit feedback
> * Quality
> * Customer photos
> * Delivery date
> * Return policy
> * Discounts
>
> AI should give a simple recommendation such as **“Best overall choice”** and clearly explain why.
>
> **2. AI Review Summary**
> Use AI to summarize verified customer reviews into simple sections such as:
>
> * Fit
> * Quality
> * Colour accuracy
> * Fabric
> * Value for money
> * Common complaints
>
> The summary should be short and easy to understand. Users should also be able to see the actual reviews behind the AI summary to maintain trust.
>
> **3. Product Confidence Card**
> Create a compact card for each wishlisted item that brings the most important purchase information into one place:
>
> * Overall rating
> * Number of reviews
> * Customer photos
> * Fit feedback
> * Quality sentiment
> * Colour accuracy
> * Size availability
> * Delivery estimate
> * Return policy
> * Current price and discount
>
> The goal is to reduce the effort users spend jumping between multiple sections or external platforms before making a purchase decision.
>
> The desired journey is:
>
> **Wishlist → Compare → Understand Reviews → Gain Confidence → Add to Bag → Purchase**
>
> Primary product metric:
> **Wishlist → Add-to-Bag Conversion**
>
> Business metric:
> **30-Day Wishlist → Purchase Conversion**
>
> Guardrail metrics:
> **Return Rate and Cancellation Rate**
>
> Design this as a realistic **Myntra mobile experience**, not an analytics dashboard. Keep the UI clean, fashion-focused, modern, lightweight, and trustworthy.
>
> Start with the **Wishlist screen**, then design the **Help Me Choose flow**, **AI Review Summary**, and **Product Confidence Card**.
